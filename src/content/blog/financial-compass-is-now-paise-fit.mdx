## Why "paise fit"?

*Financial Compass* was an accurate name for a prototype, but it felt cold, corporate, and detached. It sounded like an enterprise dashboard or an accounting package you open once a year during tax season.

Personal finance isn't an accounting chore. It's a living, daily habit. 

Just like physical fitness, keeping your money in shape requires routine, clarity, and discipline:
- Knowing exactly what is coming in and going out each week.
- Understanding your genuine net worth across all your holdings without opening seven different apps.

**paise fit** speaks directly to that mindset: **getting your money into the best shape of its life.**

## The Genesis: Born Out of Personal Frustration

This project didn't start in a boardroom. It started because I was drowning in the chaos of my own financial portfolio.

Like millions of salaried professionals and builders in India, my financial life was fragmented across disconnected islands:
- Daily living expenses and high-frequency UPI spends scattered across two bank accounts.
- Equities and Mutual Fund SIPs running on Zerodha and Groww.
- Statutory retirement reserves locked behind government portals (EPF with voluntary VPF, and a 15-year PPF account).
- Physical gold coins and Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) tracked across locker receipts and spreadsheet notes.
- Real estate commitments and family assets floating in mental math.

When I tried to find a single, calm application to unify this, I ran into two dead ends.

### Dead End 1: Foreign Personal Finance Apps
Global applications (like Monarch, Copilot, or YNAB) are beautifully designed, but they are architected exclusively for US or Western banking models (`Checking + 401(k) + Brokerage Index Fund + Mortgage`). 

They have no comprehension of:
- **High-frequency UPI flows**: dozens of ₹40, ₹150, and ₹300 micro-debits a week that turn foreign budgeting tools into a noisy nightmare.
- **Indian Sovereign Debt**: EPF statutory interest rules, Section 10(11)/(12) tax thresholds, or 15-year PPF compounding.
- **Metals & SGBs**: The semi-annual 2.50% coupon on RBI Sovereign Gold Bonds, capital-gains tax exemptions on maturity, or bullion tracking.
- **Indian Real Estate & Land**: Multi-generational property equity that forms the foundation of Indian family wealth.

### Dead End 2: The Indian Fintech Loan Trap
When you turn to popular Indian fintech apps, the reality is even worse.

Most popular domestic finance apps offer "free net worth tracking" or "credit score monitoring" as a trojan horse. Once you grant them Account Aggregator (AA) access or let them read your SMS and email inboxes, your raw financial metadata is pumped into advertising funnels. 

Within 48 hours, the spam begins: pre-approved personal loans, unsolicited credit cards, instant cash lines, and high-commission insurance policies. 

**Their business model isn't your financial fitness. Their business model is monetizing your liabilities.**

## The AI Wave: Why Privacy is Our Biggest Moat

Personal finance entered the headlines again recently as frontier AI labs (including Anthropic's Claude) introduced financial integrations and statement analysis tools.

While smarter AI analysis is exciting, it has made a glaring problem much worse: **the default solution has become uploading your most sensitive, raw financial documents to a multi-billion dollar third-party cloud.**

Ask yourself:
* *Do you really want your password-protected bank statements, medical bills, salary slips, and net worth ledger living on a commercial LLM server?*
* *Do you trust an ad-tech company or a cloud model not to train on your life's financial telemetry?*

At **paise fit**, we take the diametrically opposite approach:

### 1. 100% Client-Side Statement Parsing
When you drop your password-protected bank PDFs (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis) or your consolidated CAMS/KFintech eCAS into paise fit, **the parsing happens entirely in your local browser sandbox.** No plaintext statement or account password ever touches our servers.

### 2. Zero Telemetry & Zero Loan Spam
We don't sell personal loans, we don't push credit cards, and we don't monetize your spend data. Your financial graph belongs to you.

### 3. Deploy on Your Terms (Cloud or Self-Hosted)
While most users will appreciate our zero-configuration managed cloud instance, developers and privacy purists have complete freedom: **paise fit is architected to be self-hostable via Docker.** You can run the entire stack on your local laptop, home server, or private VPS, keeping 100% of your financial data on your own physical hardware.
